02 / THE COLLECTION

Every place, a story

A COLLECTION IN THREE CHAPTERS

Not standard house types.
A more personal point of view.

The concept gives every style of home its own narrative, while preserving one connected visual identity. These are illustrative categories for a redesign proposal, not available property listings.

01 / Country homes

Rooted in the
landscape.

Architecture that feels at home in its setting: natural materials, considered proportions and a thoughtful relationship with the garden and wider countryside.

  • Natural stone and traditional detailing
  • Gardens and views considered from the outset
  • Contemporary comfort behind timeless character

02 / Conversions

Character, made
relevant today.

Historic structures invite a different kind of design thinking. Original texture and architectural detail become part of modern living rather than decoration added afterwards.

  • Respect for original architecture
  • Modern layouts woven into existing fabric
  • Materials that allow old and new to sit together

03 / City homes

Considered living
closer to it all.

For town and city settings, good design balances privacy, connection and everyday ease, creating individual places to return to.

  • Thoughtful use of space and light
  • Finishes selected for both beauty and use
  • Architecture that acknowledges its surroundings
